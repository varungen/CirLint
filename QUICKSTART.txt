⚡ CirLint - Automated Hardware & PCB Design Verification Platform
========================================================================
Version: 1.0.0-Beta (Windows x64 Standalone)

🚀 1-MINUTE QUICKSTART:
1. Double-click 'Launch-CirLint.bat' or 'CirLint\CirLint.exe' to start.
   (No Python or external runtime installation is required!)
2. Click '⚡ Try Demo Board (Telematics Gateway)' to run an instant
   full-board verification on a sample schematic.
3. To audit your own hardware designs, click '📂 Open Project Folder...'
   and choose your Altium (.SchDoc, .PcbDoc) or KiCad (.kicad_sch) workspace.
4. Click '🚀 Run Full Board Review' to inspect:
   - Floating reset / control pins & voltage domain mismatches
   - Missing decoupling capacitors & proximity placement on PCB
   - Obsolete, single-source, and NRND lifecycle supply chain alerts
   - Instant executive PDF audit report generation

🔒 100% FREE & OFFLINE:
CirLint is completely free with unlimited schematic & PCB reviews,
multi-spin revision tracking, executive PDF reports, and local execution.
No account, subscription, or license key is required.
